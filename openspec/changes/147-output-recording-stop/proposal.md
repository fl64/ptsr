## Why

STOP can stop a preview on Files or Chop during output recording.
Further presses can leave A1 and the recording active.

## What Changes

- Stop the preview first. Then pause A1 and keep its tail in the recording.
- Save the recording with the next STOP. Keep the paused position.
- Use the same order in Assign. Keep its plan available.
- Cancel any pending Files preview on STOP.
- Name the preview step in the SMC-PAD recording hint.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- recording: make the STOP sequence available on every work screen.
- transport: cancel pending previews during an output recording.

## How to check

### Keyboard

1. Start A1. Press Ctrl+T. Open Files.
2. Press Alt+Y. Check that A1 pauses and the recording continues.
3. Press Alt+Y again. Check that the take is in hand.

### SMC-PAD

1. Start A1. Start output recording on Pads.
2. Open Chop. Start a preview. Release PAD BANK.
3. Press STOP. Check that the preview stops and A1 still plays.
4. Press STOP. Check that A1 pauses and the recording continues.
5. Press STOP again. Check that the take is in hand.

### Sound

Play the WAV that the manual check saves. Give the take time to capture the
sound after the pause. Check that the take includes this tail.
