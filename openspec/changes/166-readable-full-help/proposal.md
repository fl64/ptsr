## Why

The command grid covers part of Full Help when the SMC-PAD panel is open. The owner cannot read some rows or see all navigation controls.

## What Changes

- Give Full Help the work area at both supported sizes.
- Put the active SMC-PAD help controls in the help border.
- Keep a confirmation question in front of Full Help. Restore the previous view on back.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- command-panel: show the active panel controls in the Full Help border.
- screen-layout: give Full Help priority over the command grid.
- screen-help: show the available hold or second bank route.

## How to check

1. Open the panel and press F1 at 80x26 and 120x40.
2. Visit Help, Messages and Controller from every work screen. Scroll and close with the keyboard and SMC-PAD.
3. Check a question above Help. Cancel it, then close Help. Check the previous screen and panel.

## Sound

This change needs no WAV. The change affects layout and hints.
