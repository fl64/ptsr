## Why

Settings always tells the owner to use E2 or the arrow keys to change values. Input and Paths have other roles.

## What Changes

- Show the controls of the active Settings tab in the hint and F1.
- Explain how to browse and select a folder.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- screen-help: show the active Settings controls.

## How to check

### Keyboard

1. Press F12 and visit each tab. Open F1 on each tab.
2. On Paths, press Enter. Check how to browse and choose a folder. Press Esc.

### SMC-PAD

1. On each tab, open Controller Help. Check E1, E2 and the list buttons.
2. Open a folder picker on Paths. Follow the panel labels to browse, cancel or choose.

### Sound

This change needs no WAV. It changes the text of Settings help.
