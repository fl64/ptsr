# 48 — The pad panel in blocks, SOURCE last

## Why

The owner (2026-10-31), a screenshot of the pad edit panel with eleven rows in one list:
"maybe split it into blocks, not one list? and I suggest SOURCE goes to the very bottom".

## What changes

- The pad panel groups its rows by page: TONE, ENV, FILTER, SOURCE, each block under a
  thin line with the page name; the page of the edit is the lit block.
- SOURCE becomes the last page: `Tab` and `I` `K` walk TONE, ENV, FILTER, SOURCE, and the
  frame title names them in this order.
