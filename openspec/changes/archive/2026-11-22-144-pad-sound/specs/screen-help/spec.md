## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The pad edit shows sound shape controls

The pad edit SHALL show Pan, Fine tune, Velocity and Curve on SOUND. ENV SHALL show Hold after its current rows. The pitch readout SHALL show semitones and cents. The visible controls and values SHALL fit at 80 by 26 and 120 by 40.

#### Scenario: Sound shape values fit on both terminal sizes

- **WHEN** the owner selects SOUND or the Hold row on ENV
- **THEN** the current values and available controls are visible at both supported sizes
