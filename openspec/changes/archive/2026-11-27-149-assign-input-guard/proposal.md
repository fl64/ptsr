## Why

While the owner selects targets in Assign, three Chop actions can change
preview settings or pad gain.

## What Changes

- Ignore loop, hear as pad and normalize in Assign.
- Keep the usual Apply and Back hint for a refused action.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- chopping: keep the Assign plan separate from preview and gain controls.

## How to check

### Keyboard

1. Open Chop on a pad with a linked slice. Open Assign with P.
2. Press Alt+O, Alt+P and Ctrl+G. Check that the Apply and Back hint appears.
3. Leave with Esc. Check the loop, hear as pad and gain values.

### SMC-PAD

1. Open Chop on a pad with a linked slice. Choose put on pads.
2. Hold PAD BANK. Press P3 Menu and P14 loop play.
3. Check that the Apply and Back hint appears. Return to the target pads.
4. Leave Assign. Check the loop value.

### Sound

This change needs no new WAV. Listen to the linked pad before and after Assign.
