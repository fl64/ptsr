- "Go": the recommended options of the analysis. `Tab` turns pages (Q1.1), b3 b4 on the
  X-Session Pro turn pages in the edit (Q2.1).
- A page knob as well as the buttons (the owner: "the block switch can go on a knob and on
  a button"); no knob of the X-Session Pro is given to it by default, the owner binds one
  in F12 ("ok" to the recommended). With seven pages after 56 a part is about 18 steps of
  127.
- The pick on a new page is its first parameter, so knob 1 and `J` `L` agree on it.
- `Tab` on the pads screen with the edit closed stays `edit.param_next` as before: the
  page keys act only in the edit layer.
- Effects (delay, reverb) are not in this change. Direction noted: two buses as on the
  SP-404 (`docs/VISION.md`, section 10), a SEND page on the pad, bus parameters on their own
  object, the same page keys.
