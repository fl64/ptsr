## Why

At 80x26, the command panel covers part of the A1 grid. It can also cover projects in the picker. The knob strip leaves too little space for A1 navigation help.

## What Changes

- At 80x26, the A1 command panel uses the full work area below the title.
- At 80x26, the project picker and full Help take priority over the command panel.
- On narrow A1, show one SMC-PAD status line and use the full width for the short help.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- screen-layout: give the panel and picker distinct work areas.
- screen-help: keep short A1 navigation available with MIDI connected.

## How to check

1. Open A1 at 80x26 with the SMC-PAD connected.
2. Open the command panel. Check all four rows and the A1 title.
3. Press Ctrl+P. Check that the project list stays visible. Press Esc.
4. Close the panel. Check the step position, bank and F1 cues.
5. Open Step Edit. Check the action of a pad and the exit control.
6. Repeat at 120x40.

### Sound

This change needs no WAV. It changes screen layout.
