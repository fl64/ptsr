## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Narrow A1 keeps its navigation help with MIDI

At 80x26 with an SMC-PAD, the A1 footer SHALL use one status row and two full-width short help rows. In the overview, the title SHALL keep the bar and step counts and the step control. Short help SHALL name the bank, view and full Help controls. In Step Edit it SHALL name the pad action and the exit control.

#### Scenario: Compact A1 keeps navigation help with MIDI
- **WHEN** the owner opens A1 or enters Step Edit at 80x26 with the SMC-PAD connected
- **THEN** short help shows the navigation or edit actions for that mode
