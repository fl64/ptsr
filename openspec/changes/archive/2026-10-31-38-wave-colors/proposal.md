# 38 — The chop wave colored by frequency

## Why

The owner (2026-10-31): "how can the wave be better? it is monotone now; a gradient? but
what for? colors? what for?". The wave height already says how loud; what the eye misses
is what plays: a kick, a snare, a hat. Colored by frequency, a break reads by instrument.

## What changes

- The wave of the chop screen (F3) is colored per column by the band that stands out
  against its average over the file: the low (kick, bass) red, the mid (snare, voice)
  green, the high (hat, cymbals) blue. The picture mode (kitty) uses the same colors.
- Brightness still tells the selection: the selected slice bright, the other slices
  dimmer, outside the region the dimmest.
- Only F3: the strips of F2 and F6 and the pad waves stay as they are.

## How to verify

F3 on a break: the kicks show warm, the hats cyan, the snares light; the selected slice
is the brightest. Tests: the band analysis on a low and a high tone, the color mix, the
selection brightness.
