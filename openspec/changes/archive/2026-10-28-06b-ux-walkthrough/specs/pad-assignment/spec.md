## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Swapping with arrows
The system SHALL, while a pad is in hand, name it on the pads screen, let the arrows choose the other pad and swap with Enter; a pad hit still swaps at once.

#### Scenario: Swap without hitting
- **WHEN** pad W is in hand and the owner presses → and Enter
- **THEN** pads W and E swap, the mode of E is not changed by that Enter
