## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Jump between action groups
The system SHALL move the selection of the MIDI learn list to the previous or next group with the bank actions (PgUp/PgDn).

#### Scenario: To the chop group
- **WHEN** the MIDI tab is open and the owner presses PgDn until the CHOP group
- **THEN** the first row of the CHOP group is selected
