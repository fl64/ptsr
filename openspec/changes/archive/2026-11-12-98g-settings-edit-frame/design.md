# Design

## Decisions

- **Frame.** One frame for the F12 body in the edit color. The tabs are its pages. A narrow title keeps only the open tab, as the pad edit does with its pages.
- **Diagnostics.** The CPU and xruns line of the Audio tab moves into the frame as its first dim row.
- **Rows.** `▸` and bright text mark the selected row. Labels are lowercase and keep their width. Values keep `◀ ▶` on the selected device rows.
- **MIDI list.** Group rows stay bold. The selected group or action row is bright with `▸`. Taken actions keep the warning color.
- **Scroll.** The MIDI list keeps its scroll rule inside the frame.
