## Decision

Use three rows for the MIDI footer when a visible pad grid leaves only two rows for Rhythm. Put bank 1 knobs 3–4 and 1–2 on one row. Keep the other knob rows and the bank 2 master in order. Keep the notice on its own line.

The pad grid needs four complete rows of pads. Hiding the grid or removing an event row makes a current view unavailable. The larger footer still fits on other screens, in Pad Edit and when the pad grid is hidden.
