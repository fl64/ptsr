# 62 — With the pads hidden, the pad line shows every bank

## Why

The owner (2026-11-01): "with the pads hidden it would be good to show the banks after
all". Hidden (32), the pad line names only the open bank: which banks hold sounds and how
full they are is not seen until the grid comes back.

## What changes

- The pad line of the hidden pads shows the whole bank row, as above the grid on the pads
  screen: every bank with its fill, each in its color, the open one in brackets; the
  selected pad stays at the end of the line.
- In a narrow window the pad part is cut first, the banks stay.
- A hit pad flashes in the bank row too, at its dot in the bank's glyph — the owner: "light
  the pressed ones on their bank, as in the full view: I press, the pad lights on the
  screen" and "maybe it works for the full view too": the bank row above the grid flashes
  the same way.
