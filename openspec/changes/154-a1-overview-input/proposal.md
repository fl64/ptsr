## Why

F5 shows the A1 table. Keys that appear to browse it can change or clear the selected pad. Help still lists these pad edits.

## What Changes

- Use J/L or Left/Right for time cells and I/K or Up/Down for bars in the overview.
- Use E1 for cells and LEFT/RIGHT for bars from SMC-PAD.
- Keep pad edits on Pads. Show the keys for the current view.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- keyboard: give the A1 overview its own controls.

## How to check

### Keyboard

1. Load a pad. Open a two-bar A1 with F5. Press L and K. Check that the cursor moves.
2. Press Enter, Backspace, Tab, H, M, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Alt+G. Check the pad on F2.
3. Open F1 on A1. Find the cell and bar keys.

### SMC-PAD

1. Open A1. Turn E1 to move a cell. Press RIGHT to move a bar.
2. Hit a pad to play it. Open the panel to check edit and view actions.

### Sound

This change needs no new WAV. Check that the same pad sounds the same on F2 and F5.
