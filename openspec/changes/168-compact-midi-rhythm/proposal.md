## Why

At 80x26, the SMC-PAD knob strip can leave only two rows for Rhythm. Rhythm needs three rows, so the pattern view disappears while the pad grid is visible.

## What Changes

- Keep the Rhythm header and its two event rows visible with the pad grid.
- Fit the MIDI footer into three rows only when Rhythm needs the extra row.
- Keep both knob banks, the current context, and the safety notice.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- screen-layout: fit the Rhythm strip and MIDI controls together.

## How to check

1. With SMC-PAD connected, switch Pads from Sound to Rhythm at 80x26.
2. Check A1 absent, empty and with hits. Check the header, event rows and all pads.
3. Open Pad Edit or Help and close it. Hide and show the pad grid. Resize to 120x40 and back.

## Sound

This change needs no WAV. It changes screen layout.
